ReadAloud AI - Text to Speech for Dyslexia, ADHD, and Anyone Who Listens to Read

Reading is hard when your brain or your eyes don't cooperate. ReadAloud AI turns articles, PDFs, EPUB books, documents, emails and pasted text into natural-sounding audio, so you can listen instead of read. It works with TalkBack and plays at 0.5x to 2x speed.

Designed For:
• Readers with dyslexia who absorb information better by listening
• People with ADHD who focus better with auditory input
• Blind and low-vision users with screen reader workflows
• Anyone who'd rather listen during commutes, workouts, or chores
• Language learners practicing listening comprehension

Add Anything You Want to Hear
• Paste a web link and listen to the article
• Open PDF, EPUB, DOCX and TXT files from your phone
• Import emails from Gmail (you sign in with Google)
• Paste or type text, or import what you just copied
• Select text in another app and choose Read aloud, or share a link or file to ReadAloud

Listen in 20 Languages
• ReadAloud detects the language of your document and shows it in the player, where you can change it
• English, plus German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Czech, Greek, Finnish, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Japanese, Dutch, Polish, Romanian, Russian, Swedish, Turkish and Ukrainian, read with a ReadAloud voice when you are online
• Any other language, or no connection: ReadAloud uses a voice installed on your phone, and tells you if one is missing
• Pick a voice for each language in the player

Voices
• Ten English voices
• Optional offline voice for English (one-time download; needs Android 8 or newer and 3 GB of RAM)
• Follow along: the text scrolls with the voice as it reads

Voice Cloning
Create an AI voice from your own voice, or one you have permission to clone, by recording or uploading a clip.

Smart Playback
• Background audio, lock screen and notification controls
• Sleep timer, bookmarks and skip forward or back
• Auto-advance between chapters in books and resume where you left off
• Home screen widget to continue your latest article or read what you copied

How It Works:
1. Add content by link, file, email, pasted text or the share menu
2. Check the language and pick a voice
3. Tap play and listen anywhere
